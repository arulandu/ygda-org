You just bought your own little island smoothie bar, but, oh no! A storm destroyed all of your stock!
Spend time venturing out on your trusty boat to collect fruit on nearby islands and bring it back to blend and serve.
Fulfill orders, dodge sharks, and avoid fruit-spoiling storms!
Don't let your customers down, or you will go out of business!
