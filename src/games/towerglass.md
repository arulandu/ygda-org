Timekeeper, the sands of time are trapped in the grand tower!
It is your duty as the keeper of all time to rescue them! Climb the tower and avoid the dangers,
hazardous machines, and angry critters the tower holds! Watch your sand, flipping the glass to delay your death.
Save your progress at the friendly grandfather clocks before the sands of time within yourself settle to a halt!
Good luck, Timekeeper!
